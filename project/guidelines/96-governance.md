# Governance

## Checks before anything ships

One blue action and one accent word at most · Inter only · no literal colours outside the
tokens · glass has the lens filter and a photograph or blooms behind it · no type or glass on
a person · circles are circles · a focus ring on every interactive part · no em dash, no
exclamation mark, no banned word · logo untouched · every grid row full · no pricing,
compliance or user-count claims · no vendor or assistant names.
