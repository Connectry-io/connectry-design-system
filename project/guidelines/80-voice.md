# Voice

Sober, warm, specific. Short sentences. One accent word per headline, and the headline ends
with a full stop. "It" is the subject more often than "we". We only say what we did once: we
run ours on it first.

## Never

Exclamation marks. Em dashes. Hype words: delve, unlock, seamless, empower, journey, leverage,
landscape, game-changing, supercharge, revolutionary, cutting edge, next generation,
AI-powered, intelligent, smart.

Pricing, tiers, compliance or certification claims, user counts, "trusted by", revenue, any
specific industry. Any assistant or vendor name: say "wherever you already work". "Family"
alone. A business door, organisations, methodology.

## Lines that carry the voice

It takes care. · Ready to use. · Only what you choose to share. · On whatever they
use. · For whoever comes next. · Mail read, calendar moved, coffee still hot. · It remembers. ·
It holds your people. · Some things are yours. Some things you share. · Your year, prepared. ·
It builds. · It knows why. · Passed down, not lost. · What the table knows, it keeps. ·
Remembers. Takes care. Passes on. · It's already done. · Wherever you already work. ·
We run ours on it first.

## Values, in order

It remembers you, why and not just what · It takes care · It holds your people · It
passes on · It builds · Yours, on every surface · Safe by structure, not by a policy page.

## Shape of a page

One idea per surface. Restraint that looks expensive. If it could be a generic startup
template or a PowerPoint end slide, it is wrong.

## UI copy

A state is a word, never a colour alone: Done, Live, Selected. An error is a plain sentence in
`muted` beneath the field. A button says what it does in two or three words. No emoji in UI
copy.
