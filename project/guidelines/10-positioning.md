# Positioning

One brain per person. And one for your people. One memory for the whole of a life, on every
surface the person already uses: their assistant, their messages, their mail. Text, pictures
and everything they connect. It grows with them, takes care of what it can, and outlives
the moment. It remembers why, not just what. And it arrives with the applications ready to use:
the circle, the trip, health, learning, money. Internally those are the brain's spaces.
Nothing to set up; the complexity is ours, not theirs. Connectry is a memory and applications
company, not a memory alone.

## The wedge

Host neutrality: one shared memory for people who each use a different assistant, dad on one,
mum on another, you on a third. No first party can do that, because their memory is their
lock-in. In every circle a person shares only what they choose; mail is never one of those
things. It passes on: full export and a legacy contact at launch, so what a circle knows
outlives the phone, the job, the app and the person. Yours, on your own keys; it leaves with
you, never with the app.

There is no app of our own and no chat of our own. The conversation stays in the host and the
brain reaches it through one connector. The ask bar in the film is a film device for that
exchange, not a product screen.

## The two doors

Described, never named as products.

- **For you.** The individual, the whole of a life in scope, work included. "Your year,
  prepared."
- **For your people.** Circles: one for each group, a flat share, a five-a-side team,
  grandparents on a video call, a parents' group, a couple, friends on a trip, the cafe, the
  freelancer, the small team. "Some things are yours. Some things you share."

Never "family" alone; the circle is always wider. No business door, no organisations, no
methodology, no pricing on anything public.

## The three words

Remembers. Takes care. Passes on. Each one is something already built and running.

Retired on 2026-09-23: "Acts before you ask" and "It acts before you ask". Replaced by "Takes care" and
"It takes care."

Retired on 2026-09-20: "It was up before you", in every form. It is never written again.
Retired as slogans or display lines: "It knows you." and "Stop briefing your AI."

## Slogan

"Your brain. Wherever you work." It sits under the wordmark on the cover slide, the one-pager
and the social cards. Not on the film endcard.

## Proof

Only numbers we can stand behind: 164 consecutive daily briefings · 1,715 audited calls by
four people · built for a team of 25. Never user counts, revenue or pricing.
