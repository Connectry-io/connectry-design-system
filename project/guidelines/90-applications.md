# Applications

## Web

The website is **Chapters, dark** (**WebChaptersDark**). Its light edition, **WebChaptersLight**,
is a known version, not a toggle.

**Hero.** **WebHero**: one memory building over a people-first film that plays once, three
moments, the layers sinking into the waitlist field and the logo forming above it.

**The page.** What Connectry is · Memory (film) · Circles · Where it lives (film) · It takes
care (film) · Yours · Start your circle (the last film, full screen). The glass moments are in
**WebSections**. One action: Join the waitlist, in the hero, the menu and the close.

**Nav.** **WebNav**: no logo until you scroll; desktop has Contact us and the three-line menu;
phones have only the centred lockup.

**Footer.** **WebFooter**: one line on the faded end of the last film, the copyright and three
social glyphs, no logo.

**Phones.** One left-aligned column, no sideways scrolling, circles as rows, questions allowed
two lines in a taller bar, the lighter hero film.

**Copy.** Connection types are named by kind (your messages, mail, calendar, voice notes,
photos, your own assistant), never by product. No names, no numbers, no pricing, no diagrams.

**References.** The split **SiteHero** with the form above the fold, the six-chapter **Website**
method and **AskHero** stay for CTA and campaign pages.

## Product surfaces

The same tokens and components. One idea per screen. Lists as hairline rows. The brain speaks
in a message panel; a built thing appears as a doc preview; a circle speaks in a circle panel
with the members row and never exposes a member's private memory.

## Slides

16:9 at 1920 by 1080. Margin 86, lockup 36px top left, section label in caps beneath it, page
number bottom right, `ground` behind, glass only where a panel is needed, no bullet layouts.

Eighteen layouts: cover, agenda, divider, one idea, three points, proof, column chart, line
chart, bar chart before and after, part to whole, timeline, comparison, two column, quote,
full bleed with glass, full bleed with one line, team, closing (the animated lockup, centred,
no slogan).

Charts are native: accent series `chart-accent`, comparison `chart-comparison`, further steps
`chart-step-1` and `chart-step-2`, hairline gridlines only, the value on the mark, a source
line at the foot, one axis.

## Social

Forty-seven approved cards across 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 and 9:16: one line (a), the brain speaks (b),
type on the ground (c), a morning (d), numbers (e), comparison (f), carousel (g), stories (h),
circles (k). See **SocialCard** for the measured anatomy: the full lockup with its name bottom
left at 4.6 percent of the width, the slogan as the one dark-plate glass chip bottom right on
the lockup's line, 6.5 percent margins, the footer 7.5 percent up (19 percent on stories). Glass
panels on the open third only. A social still may carry one ask pair, never a list.

## Collateral

A one-page overview in A4 and Letter; an email signature as a table with the mark hosted as a
PNG; the slide template with editable native charts.
