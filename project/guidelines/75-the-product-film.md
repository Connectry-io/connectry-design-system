# The product film grammar

The 75-second product film set the moving-picture grammar. Everything here was measured on the
cut, not guessed.

**Opening.** Home first: a kitchen at dawn, then friends on a Sunday, then the thesis typed
over an empty table. No warp zoom, no drone descent as an opener, no artificial camera move of
any kind. Each shot carries its own motion and a 2 percent settle push so a cut has a breath.

**The three worlds.** The story is agnostic across home, the people you love, and the work you
do. The dashboard carries one item of each; the asks run through your morning, your family,
your admin, your work and your friends; the trip room is the circle's plan. The voice names
the three worlds once and never lists product features.

**The product surface.** Never a full-frame app. The board and the trip room are the product's
own HTML at real scale in the brand glass over the person's own photo. The host's chat is
replicated to the pixel, toned so it never reads as white: page `film-host-page`, paper
`film-host-paper`, a 13 percent edge vignette. Connected tools carry their real marks,
Connectry last in the list. No assistant is named on screen or in the voice.

**Type over people.** The film headline is 107.5px, left 132px, bottom 96 to 107px, typed at
about 24 characters a second and resolving by 60 percent of the shot, solid caret, the accent
word in `blue-light`. It sits on the quiet third and never on a face.

**Macro register.** Perspective 1600, rotationY 8 to 10 degrees with the near edge left, scale
1.30 to 1.66, a living camera on every shot, no hold of ten frames or more. The far edge
defocuses at 3px. Between two angles of the same panel there is never a hard cut: a
rack-focus hand-over of six frames, the outgoing angle softening to 7px and fading while the
incoming arrives from 6px to sharp, both cameras still moving. Cuts between different content
stay hard, on the beat.

**Defocus and bloom.** Small radii only: 3px on a macro edge, 9px behind a device over a
plate, 5px on a blurred double. Blooms at 8 percent or under, except the spectacle, the film's
one colour event.

**Endcard.** The coda's last frame dissolves into the daylight plate over 18 frames, no dip to
black; the two brackets fly in from opposite corners and land on the music's accent; the plate
sinks to `endcard-black`, the mark slides into the lockup, the wordmark wipes in on a soft
mask, one specular sweep, then the slogan beneath at 34px with the second sentence in
`blue-light`. The lockup's glyph extents are optically centred at 960: the wordmark carries a
right bearing, so centre by glyphs, not by box. The slogan on the endcard is the film's own;
the brand endcard elsewhere stays the lockup alone.

**Sound.** One voice, dry and warm, no product nouns beyond the name, recorded as one
continuous performance and split at its own silences so tone and level never jump between
lines. The bed rides 8dB under the voice while it speaks and a steady -20 dBFS in the gaps,
250ms pre-roll, 1s hold. Conformed to 126 BPM: drums at 13.333s, a hard mute at 58.333 to
59.048, the accent on the strike at 70.583s cut unstretched from the track's own final hit.
Web at -14 LUFS and -1 dBTP, broadcast at -23 LUFS.

**Gates, every cut.** Frame count by ffprobe, one pixel probe per segment, no dead holds, no
one-frame swaps, no stepped motion, isolated pops only inside 1.2x tracking motion, beat phase
within 15ms, accent within 1ms, loudness measured on the file.

**Never.** A warp zoom, a drone descent as an opener, a full-frame app, an assistant named, a
generic icon standing in for a real product, a blur radius past 9px, a pure white page, an ask
bar on a face, a boxed answer, a hype word, an exclamation mark, an em dash. A glass panel
hugs its content and carries enough of it: never empty glass under a dashboard, never a room
that is only a photo and a composer.
