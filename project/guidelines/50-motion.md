# Motion

Things arrive once. Nothing loops. The website's hero film plays once and holds its last frame. The **Motion**
card plays every moment below; press Play again.

`cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)` for anything that arrives or comes to rest. `ease` at 160ms for
hover and state. `ease-in-out` at 1.1s for the sheen. The brackets use
`cubic-bezier(.16,.8,.24,1)`.

Rows inside glass land 0.6s each, 6px, 350ms apart, after their panel.

Durations: 160ms state · 200ms toggle · 700ms wordmark reveal · 900ms brackets · 1.2s glass
arrival · 1.1s sheen · 70 to 90s blooms. These are not tokens; they are the values above, used
as written.

**Endcard and closing slide.** The animated lockup alone, centred, no slogan and no other
type, over a moving city plate under a 28 percent veil. The brackets travel in from opposite
corners and decelerate home over 0.9s from 0.2s; the wordmark reveals left to right over 0.7s
from 1.0s.

**Film.** 5s clips, one slow camera move, no cuts inside a clip; glass on film arrives after
the first 0.4s. Hard cuts on a beat between clips.

**Reduced motion.** The glass is simply there, the blooms hold still, the lockup is shown
complete, film keeps its poster frame.

**The website hero.** See **WebHero** for the timeline. The line of light draws out over 1.5s
and swells into the bar; morphs run 1.4s on `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.25,1)`; arrivals settle on
`cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`; content cross-fades through a small blur; questions type at about
34ms a letter, each letter settling in; the answer's last word glows over 1.8s and breathes
slowly; the logo forms with the brackets drifting sideways out of a soft blur over 1.8s and the
name wiping in left to right over 1.9s. Section moments on the site play once on scroll.

**Edges.** On the dark edition, film chapters dissolve into the page: 2.5 percent at the top, 9 at
the bottom; the hero and the last film fade into the page beneath. The light edition keeps clean
film edges, with no fade into the grey ground. Page sections change surface over 30 percent of
their height.

The earlier three-shot hero loop is retired with the split hero. Clips in the design system play
once and replay when pressed.

**Never.** Loops, pulses, shimmer, parallax, bounce, anything on scroll except one arrival,
motion inside the mark. Typing only inside the hero's glass, a wipe only for the wordmark, a
dissolve only between film shots and at film edges.
