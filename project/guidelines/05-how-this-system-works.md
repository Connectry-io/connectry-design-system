# How this system works

Everything Connectry makes, from a slide to the product, is built from the same small set of
parts in the same order. Read the cards top to bottom and you can make anything.

**1. Tokens.** Colour, type, spacing, radii, shadows. Use them by name (`ink`, `muted`, `blue`,
`space-6`, `radius-panel`), never as raw values.

**2. The glass primitive.** **GlassPanel** is the only surface that floats. Light plate, dark
plate, or on the ground. Every panel in the system is this primitive with content inside it.

**3. Panels.** What the brain says, in glass: the morning (**MorningWidget**), a circle
(**CircleCard**), an answer (**ReplyPanel**), a notification (**NotificationGlass**), a choice
to share (**ShareToCircle**), a kept fact (**MemoryCard**). Each has the same anatomy: a head row
with a label and a state word, one line or a title, then rows, a well, a doc preview or the
members row.

**4. Surfaces.** Panels placed on a picture or on the ground: the **Dashboard**, the **Board**,
**HostChat**, **PhoneScreens**.

**5. Chapters.** A surface, a picture and one line make a chapter. The site is six of them, one
feeling each (**Website**). A slide is a chapter at 16:9 (**Deck**); a social card is a chapter
at 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16 (**SocialCard**).

**6. Film.** The same pictures, moving: the clips, the hero loop, the 75-second product film
(**FilmLibrary**).

## To make something new

1. Pick the feeling: relief, belonging, being known, or something that lasts.
2. Pick a picture from Imagery or Circles with an open third for the glass.
3. Write one line in the voice, one accent word, a full stop.
4. Put at most one panel on the open third, never on a face. Choose the plate from
   **GlassContrast**.
5. Check it against "Before anything ships" in the brand book.

## Words used in this system

**Plate**, the tint of a glass panel (light or dark). **Open third**, the part of a picture
with no face in it. **Ground**, the `ground` grey with its two blooms. **Rail**, the left edge a
line sits on. **State word**, the word that says what happened: Live, Done, Shared, Kept,
Drafted, Needs you. **Circle**, any group of people sharing a memory, never "family" alone.
