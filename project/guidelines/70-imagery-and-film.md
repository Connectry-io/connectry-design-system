# Imagery and film

Real people in real moments. One person or one circle, one moment, one line on the left rail.
Cinematic, natural light, warm neutral grade with subtle grain, 35mm, shallow depth, warm skin
with a hint of cool blue in the shadows. No screen facing camera, no eye contact with the
lens, mixed ethnicity and age, no industry cues, uniforms, logos or readable screens.

**Two sets, one shoot.** *The individual*, twenty scenes: dawn city, curtain, kitchen morning,
phone in hand, morning crowd, lift doors, train sunrise, rain on glass, lobby walk, colleagues
laughing, hands typing, glass corridor, coffee pouring, meeting-room window, golden crossing,
cyclist, eyes and screen, desk at dusk, blue hour city, night walk. *The circles*, ten scenes:
grandparents on a call, the flat share, five-a-side, the school gate, Sunday sofa, the cafe
opening, friends on the platform, the toast, the freelancer, the small team. The individual
scenes carry the first door, the circle scenes the second.

**Composition.** Subject in the right two thirds; the left rail free for the line; room beside
the people for a glass panel.

**The interference rule.** Type and glass never sit on a person's face. The line takes the
open third, the panel takes the open third beside the people. The one exception is the ask
bar, which may cross the body below the shoulders because it is the person's own sentence; it
never crosses a face. If a frame has no open third, choose another frame or another crop;
shorten copy before widening a panel.

**Pairings.** "It takes care." (dawn city, curtain, kitchen) · "It remembers."
(crowd, train, rain glass) · "It knows your team." (lift, corridor, laptop laugh) · "It
builds." (hands typing, coffee, the small team) · "It knows why." (meeting-room window) ·
"Wherever you already work." (crossing, cyclist, cafe) · "It's already done." (dusk desk, blue
hour, night walk) · "It holds your people." (flat share, five-a-side, school gate, platform) ·
"Passed down, not lost." (grandparents, the toast) · "Some things are yours. Some things you
share." (Sunday sofa) · "Your year, prepared." (freelancer, cafe).

**Generation.** Stills with GPT Image 2.5 at high, 2k, 16:9, two candidates per scene; motion
with Kling 3.0, 5s, the pick as start image, a camera move written into every prompt. The
prompt skeleton: "Cinematic still, [one person, one moment], [light], [placement: subject
right half, left third open for type]. Warm neutral grade, natural light, subtle grain,
[35|50]mm, shallow depth, no text, no logos, photorealistic." `imagery/manifest.json` in the
brand repository holds the job ids and prompts for regeneration in the same system.

**People first.** Every shot on the website opens on people: one person or a circle. The city
is only ever behind them.

**Quality.** Film on the site is encoded from the full-resolution originals at 1920 wide, lightly
compressed (CRF about 20), with a light sharpen; never from the small preview copies. Stills at
1200 wide or more. Blur on the page is a bug, not a style.

**Never.** Stock AI imagery, diagrams, node graphics, sparkles, illustrations, icon grids,
posed smiles at camera, handshakes, teal grades, HDR, type on a face, centred type on a
photograph, more than one line on a photograph, a nuclear family alone as the picture of "your
people".
