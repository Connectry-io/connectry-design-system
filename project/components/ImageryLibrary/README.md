# ImageryLibrary

The whole photographic set: twenty scenes of the individual, ten of the circles, and an alternate
take for each individual scene. Every picture is an upload in the **Imagery** or **Circles**
asset group, named by its scene number.

Each scene carries the line it is approved for. Use the pairing; do not put a line on a scene it
was not shot for.

Grade, composition, the interference rule and the generation recipe are in the Imagery and film
section.
