# WebNav

The website bar, in both editions.

- **Over the hero.** No logo. On desktop: Contact us, a quiet outline button, then the three-line menu. On phones: nothing.
- **Scrolled.** The lockup arrives at 24px on the left (desktop) or centred (phones); the bar turns to glass: graphite at 55 percent with a 24px blur on the dark edition, `ground` at 74 percent on the light one.
- **The menu.** Desktop only. Three thin lines that turn into a close mark; a glass panel with Memory, Circles, Where it lives, It takes care, Yours, Watch the film and Join the waitlist in `blue`.
- **Contact us.** Opens a glass dialog: name, email, message, Send. Dark glass on the dark edition.
- The lockup sits in a flex box so its cap height is centred on the button's centre line.

The earlier **Nav**, with links and a pill, stays as a reference.
