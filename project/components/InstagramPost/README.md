# InstagramPost

Feed posts at 1:1 (1080) and 4:5 (1080 × 1350), shown in the feed frame.

- Every post carries the full lockup bottom left and the glass slogan chip bottom right; the
  profile picture is the mark on ink.
- Captions: one or two sentences in the voice, no hashtags wall, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- The profile grid crops 4:5 to 3:4: the 6.5 percent margin keeps the lockup and chip inside it.
- Carousels: up to six pages from the **SocialLibraryCircles** g-set, page count top right.
