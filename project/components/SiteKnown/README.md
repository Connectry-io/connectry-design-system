## Recipe

1. **Head.** "It remembers why." 72px. Lede: "Not just what happened, but the reason it was set.

   friend."
2. **Stage.** The ground with its blooms, inset to the gutter. The **AskBar** at 640px:
   "Is Dad's check-up still Thursday?"
3. **Answer.** 34px `ink`, the accent on the part that is new: "Thursday, 9:30. Leo's driving
   him." Beneath it, the reason in `muted` 14px (see **ReplyPanel** for the full answer).
