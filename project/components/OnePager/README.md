# OnePager

The one-page overview, A4 and Letter, built on the same gutter of 7 percent of the width.

Anatomy, top to bottom: the mark, the display line, the lead, one photograph with a single
line on it, the three-word proof in three columns, and a hairline foot carrying the slogan and
the domain.

The three columns are always Remembers · Takes care · Passes on, in that order, each
with one sentence.

Nothing else goes on it: no pricing, no tiers, no compliance badges, no logos of tools, no QR
code, no team photographs, no "trusted by" row.

It exports flat to PDF with the photograph at 300ppi and the type live, never as an image.
