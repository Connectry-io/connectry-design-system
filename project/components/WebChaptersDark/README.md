# WebChaptersDark

The website as decided: Chapters, dark. The A1 hero (**WebHero**), then eight beats on graphite pages between full-bleed film chapters, desktop and phone.

## The page

1. **Hero.** **WebHero**.
2. **What Connectry is.** "One memory for your life and your people." One sentence anyone understands.
3. **Memory.** Film chapter, the platform. "It remembers why, not just what." An ask with a follow up.
4. **Circles.** Graphite page, one step lighter. "Some things are yours. Some things you share." Four circles, cards on desktop, rows on phones.
5. **Where it lives.** Film chapter, the night walk, centred. "No new app. It lives where you already talk." The sources grid and the one memory beneath it.
6. **It takes care.** Film chapter, the kitchen. "The small things, handled before they grow." This morning.
7. **Yours.** Graphite page. "It knows the whole story. You decide who sees which part." Keys, export, legacy contact.
8. **Start your circle.** The toast, full screen: the field, the film behind a button, the sign-off line; the footer rests on the film's faded end.

## Dark, done well

- `ground` in the Site dark theme is warm graphite `#0e0f11`, never black; `surface` `#121316` is the one lighter step, blended in and out over 30 percent of the section.
- Type in off-white `#f3f3f5`: headlines full, body 74, secondary 60, the footer 56 percent.
- One blue on dark: `blue-light` for eyebrows and accents. No other tints.
- White accents: a 40px white hairline above each page heading; icons in soft white.
- A barely there pool of blue light (2 percent) at the top of plain pages. Nothing stronger.
- Film chapters dissolve into the page: 2.5 percent at the top, 9 percent at the bottom. The hero and the last film fade into the page.

## Phones

One left-aligned column. The film sits above its words and fades into the page; glass cards run full width. Circles become rows. No sideways scrolling anywhere, checked at 390 and 360.

Dark is the website's context, not a brand switch: everything else stays light first. The light edition is **WebChaptersLight**.
