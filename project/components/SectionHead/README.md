# SectionHead

A two-column split: the headline left at `headline`, the lead right at `lead`. A `label`
above, a hairline over the whole block, `space-12` from here to the content beneath.

The headline breaks at 14ch, ends with a full stop and carries at most one accent word in
`blue`. The lead runs to 56ch and no further.

Below 900 the two columns stack, headline first.

This is the only section header the system has. No centred headings, no eyebrow-plus-title
stacks, no rules above and below.
