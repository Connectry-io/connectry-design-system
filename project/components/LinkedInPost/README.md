# LinkedInPost

LinkedIn in two formats: the single image at 16:9 (1600 × 900, cropped to 1.91:1 in the feed)
and the document carousel (4:5 pages exported as one PDF).

- The full lockup and the glass slogan chip on every image and every carousel page.
- On 16:9 keep the line inside the middle 1.91:1 band; the footer sits within it.
- The company page avatar is the mark on ink; the tagline is the slogan.
- Copy: one or two sentences, no hashtags wall, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
