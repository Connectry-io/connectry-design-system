The page, as a method

Six chapters. One feeling each.

The site is not a list of features. It is a short film you scroll: each chapter carries one feeling and one line, built from the components in this system. To make a page, pick the chapters, keep their order, and fill each recipe card.

1One feeling per chapter, in this order: relief, belonging, being known, something that lasts.
2Chapters alternate: film or picture full bleed, then the grey ground, then a picture again.
3Each chapter has one line with one accent word, and at most one glass panel.
4No names, no numbers, no proof row, no pricing, no vendor or assistant names.
5One action on the whole page: Join the waitlist, in the nav and at the close.
6Gutter max(20px, 6.9vw); 120px between ground chapters; picture chapters 720 high.
