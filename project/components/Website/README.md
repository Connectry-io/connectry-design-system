# Website

> Reference. The website is now **WebChaptersDark**, with **WebChaptersLight** as its light edition. The six chapter cards below stay as a method and a source of recipes.

The landing page as a method: six chapters, one feeling each, each built from components in
this system. The chapters are cards of their own (**SiteHero**, **SiteRelief**,
**SiteBelonging**, **SiteKnown**, **SiteLasts**, **SiteFilmWaitlist**), each with its recipe.

## The method

1. **One feeling per chapter,** in this order: relief, belonging, being known, something that
   lasts. The close gathers all four.
2. **Rhythm.** Film or picture full bleed (720 high), then the grey ground with the blooms, then a
   picture again. Never two ground chapters without a picture between them except Known and the
   close.
3. **One line per chapter** with one accent word (`blue` on the ground, `blue-light` on film),
   ending with a full stop. A lede of two sentences at most beside it, 360 wide.
4. **At most one glass panel per chapter,** on the open third, never on a face, never over the
   line.
5. **One action on the page:** Join the waitlist, in the nav and in the close.
6. **Nothing that is not marketing:** no names, no founders, no numbers, no proof row, no pricing,
   no vendor or assistant names.

## Making a new page

Start from the chapters, not from a blank grid. Swap the picture for another from Imagery or
Circles with the same open third, rewrite the line in the voice, keep the component. Measure with
the gutter `max(20px, 6.9vw)`; ground chapters have 120px above and below.
