# SiteHero

> Reference. The website now opens with **WebHero**. Keep this split hero for CTA pages and campaigns where the form must sit above the fold.

Chapter one of the site, and the first five seconds of the brand: what it is, why it matters to
you, and the one action, all above the fold. Relief.

## Layout

A split hero on the grey ground with the two blooms, 800 high at 1280.

- **Nav.** 84px. The ink lockup at 30px, Product · Your people · Film in `body` 15px, one
  **Pill** "Join the waitlist". Over a picture the same nav in white.
- **Copy, left, 88px gutter.**
  1. Eyebrow: a small ground-glass pill with the live dot, "Opening circles, a few at a time".
  2. Headline, 80px, `-0.04em`: "One memory. For all of it." with "all" as the accent word.
  3. Sub, 19px, 470 wide, two lines: "Your life, your people and your work in one memory that
     grows with you. It lives where you already talk, and it takes care."
  4. The **WaitlistForm** field and pill as one shape, 480 wide, with the note "One email when
     your circle can start. Nothing else." The action is in the hero, not a scroll away.
  5. Three promises on a hairline: Nothing to set up · Only what you choose to share · On your own
     keys. Promises, never numbers or logos.
- **Stage, right, 560 wide.** The silent three-shot hero loop in a radius-28 frame with the stage
  shadow. The **MorningWidget** (dark plate) overlaps the frame's left edge at 330px, and a
  **NotificationGlass** from a circle sits bottom right. Never on a face.

## Motion, once

Copy lands top to bottom (0.1 to 0.6s, 6px). The widget arrives at 0.3s (1.2s), its rows land at
1.2, 1.55 and 1.9s, the circle notification arrives at 2.6s. Then everything holds still; only
the film loops. Reduced motion shows the end state.

## Why this layout

The value proposition, the proof that it works (the product, live) and the action share the
first screen; one field and one button keep the ask small; the film supplies warmth without
putting type on a face.

## Phone

One column: nav with the lockup at 26px and the pill, the copy, the form full width, then the
stage at 4:5 with the widget inside the frame.
