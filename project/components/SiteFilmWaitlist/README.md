# SiteFilmWaitlist

The close: the film behind one play button, the waitlist, the footer.

## Recipe

1. **Film.** The product film (see **FilmLibrary**) in a 16:9 frame inset to the gutter, radius
   18, the poster from the kitchen overture, "The film · 75 seconds, sound on" bottom left. It
   never autoplays with sound.
2. **Waitlist.** On the ground with the blooms: "Start your circle." 72px, the lede "We are
   opening a few circles at a time. Leave your email and we will write when yours can start.",
   the **WaitlistForm** field, the note "One email when your circle can start. Nothing else."
3. **Footer.** The ink lockup at 18px, Privacy and Contact. Nothing else: no names, no numbers,
   no social icons.
