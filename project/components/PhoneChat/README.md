# PhoneChat

The board arriving in a phone host, in the host's own dark theme. The same card, the same glass,
the same words as the desktop: only the host's chrome changes.

## Anatomy

The thread title is the person's brain ("Sam's brain"), never the host's name. The person's ask
in a blue bubble, the brain's reply in the host's own bubble, the board card with the glass sheet
over the person's plate, one more line of what was done, then three suggestions as quiet chips.
The composer carries "Say what should change" and the Connectry chip.

## On film

The phone stands in front of a plate defocused at 9px, the film's maximum behind a device. No
screen faces the camera in the photography; the phone here is a product shot, not a scene.

## Never

A host's logo or name, a notification badge, emoji in the thread, a second board card in one
reply.
