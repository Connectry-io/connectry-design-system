# SocialCard

The anatomy every social card follows, measured on a real card.

- **Line.** One line in white Inter 500 with one accent word in `blue-light` and a full stop; an
  optional second line at 42 percent size in white 78 percent.
- **Lockup.** The full lockup, with the name, bottom left: white on pictures, ink on the ground.
  4.6 percent of the card's width (3.2 percent on 16:9). Never the mark alone.
- **Glass chip.** "Your brain. Wherever you work." in dark-plate glass (74 to 62 percent), bottom
  right, vertically centred on the lockup's line. On the ground: the ground glass.
- **Margin.** 6.5 percent of the width on every side; the footer line sits 7.5 percent up.
- **Stories.** On 9:16 the footer lifts to 19 percent so the reply bar never covers it.
- **Panels.** At most one glass panel on the open third; never on a face, never overlapping the
  line.

approved card.
