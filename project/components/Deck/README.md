# Deck

The master deck: eighteen layouts at 1920 × 1080, rendered from the slide template. Each is an
upload in the **Slides** asset group (`01.jpg` to `18.jpg`), in the order below.

01 Cover · 02 Agenda · 03 Divider · 04 One idea · 05 Three points · 06 Proof · 07 Column chart ·
08 Line chart · 09 Before and after · 10 Part to whole · 11 Timeline · 12 Comparison · 13 Two
column · 14 Quote · 15 Full bleed with glass · 16 Full bleed, one line · 17 Team · 18 Closing

## Using it

Build a new deck from the Slides artifact type with this design system selected, and take these
eighteen as the layout reference: margin 86, lockup 36px top left, section label beneath it,
page number bottom right (the cover unnumbered), `ground` behind, glass only where a panel is
needed. The **Slide** component shows the live anatomy.

## The three words on slides

Remembers. Takes care. Passes on. The agenda, one idea and three points layouts carry
them, in that order.

## Never

Bullet layouts, a title-and-three-bullets slide, a stock photo, a logo wall, a chart drawn as a
picture, the slogan on the closing slide.
