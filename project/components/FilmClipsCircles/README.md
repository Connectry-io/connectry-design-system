# FilmClipsCircles

Circles: Five-a-side, The toast, Colleagues. Each is a five-second 16:9 clip from the **Film**
asset group, playing once here; press to replay.

## Rules for every clip

- One slow camera move written into the prompt, no cuts inside a clip.
- Real people, candid, never looking at the lens. Glass arrives after the first 0.4s and never
  sits on a face.
- The poster frame is the matching still in Imagery or Circles.
- Hard cuts on the beat between clips; the grade stays warm-neutral, blacks lifted slightly.

Full-resolution files: `media/film/<name>.mp4` and the Film asset group.
