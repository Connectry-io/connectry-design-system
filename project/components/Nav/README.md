# Nav

> Reference. The website uses **WebNav**.

The site bar: the full lockup, three links, one pill.

- **Lockup.** 30px high on desktop, 26 on phones; ink on the ground, white over film. Always the
  lockup, never the mark alone.
- **Links.** Product · Your people · Film, 15px, 32px apart, `body` on the ground, white on film.
- **Action.** One **Pill**, "Join the waitlist", 42px.
- **Scrolled.** 68px, `glass--nav`: 62 percent white, 18px blur, a hairline beneath, no rim and
  no sheen.
- **Phone.** Lockup and pill only; the links move into the page.
