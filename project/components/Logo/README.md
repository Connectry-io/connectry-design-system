# Logo

Two brackets on a 100 unit grid, stroke 15, gap 6, and the wordmark "Connectry" in Inter
SemiBold at -0.03em converted to paths.

The mark reads as a crop mark, a frame around what matters. It carries no meaning that needs
explaining and it is never explained.

## Lockup

Mark height equals the cap height (0.7275em); gap is 0.6 cap. The horizontal lockup viewBox is
674.88 by 145.51; the wordmark alone is 558.47 by 145.51. A stacked lockup exists for square
spaces.

## Colourways

`ink` on light, white on dark and on photography, `blue` only on `surface` or `ground`.

## Files

Outlined masters are uploads in the **Logo** asset group: the horizontal lockup in ink and
white, the mark in ink and white, and the favicon (the mark on `blue` at `radius-card`). App
icon is the mark on blue as a rounded square; the avatar is the same on a circle. Use the
masters in production; the preview sets the wordmark live so it renders without the outlines.

## Never

Stretch, rotate, outline, gradient, shadow, change the gap, add a symbol, recolour, animate
the mark on its own, or place the logo inside glass. The endcard animation is the one
exception, and it animates the lockup.
