# NotificationGlass

What the brain says when the person is not looking at it: on the lock screen and as a desktop
banner, one sentence about something already done.

## Structure

- **Sender.** The mark in white on a 30px `blue` tile, radius 8. The only place the mark sits on
  blue in the product.
- **Head.** "Connectry" or the circle's name ("Lisbon · four of you") in 12px 600, time in
  `muted`.
- **Line.** 13px on the phone, 14px on the desktop. Past tense: read, moved, drafted, told.
- **Actions.** Desktop only, two text links at most: Open, Later.

Always the dark plate (the wallpaper behind is never known). Radius 20 on the phone, 18 on the
desktop. At most two at once, stacked with 8px between.

## Never

A badge count, "urgent", an exclamation mark, a question the person has to answer to clear it,
an assistant's name as the sender.
