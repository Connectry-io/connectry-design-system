# Motion

Every moving thing in the system, live, with its timing. Press "Play again" to see them once more.

## The moments

- **Glass arrives.** 1.2s, rises 14px and resolves from 10px of blur, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`;
  one diagonal sheen follows in 1.1s.
- **Rows land.** 0.6s each, 6px, 350ms apart, after the panel.
- **The endcard.** Brackets travel in from opposite corners over 0.9s from 0.2s,
  `cubic-bezier(.16,.8,.24,1)`; the wordmark is revealed left to right over 0.7s from 1.0s.
- **State.** 160ms `ease`; toggles 200ms.
- **Blooms.** Drift over 70 to 90s.

- **The website hero.** The line of light, the bar, the answer, the memory layers and the logo
  forming: see **WebHero**, which plays it live.

## Rules

Everything plays once. Reduced motion shows the end state: the glass is there, the lockup is
complete, the blooms hold still, film keeps its poster. The website hero film plays once and holds its last frame.

## Never

Loops, pulses, shimmer, parallax, bounce, scroll-linked motion, motion inside the mark. Typing,
the wordmark wipe and film dissolves live only where 50-motion allows them.
