# CircleCard

How a circle speaks: a glass panel over the circle's own photograph with the circle's name and
time, one line, what it holds and who is in it.

## Structure

- **Head.** The circle and its time or its year as `label`, a state word right.
- **Title.** One line, 20px, full stop. No accent word inside a circle panel.
- **Body.** Up to two hairline rows (label, line, state) or one doc preview for a kept thing.
- **Foot.** The members row (26px circles, fixed size, never squeezed), a count in words, and
  the sharing word: Shared, Kept, Passed down.

## Rules

Circles are wider than any one household: a team, a flat, a trip, grandparents, a cafe. The panel shows
only what the circle chose to share, never a member's private memory, never mail. Dark plate on
dark or busy pictures, light plate on bright, calm ones.
