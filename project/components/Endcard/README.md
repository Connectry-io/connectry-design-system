# Endcard

The animated lockup alone, centred, over a moving city plate under a 28 percent veil. No
slogan and no other type; the brand endcard is the lockup and nothing else.

The two brackets travel in from opposite corners and decelerate home over 0.9s from 0.2s on
`cubic-bezier(.16,.8,.24,1)`. The wordmark reveals left to right over 0.7s from 1.0s. One
specular sweep, then stillness. A faint brand-blue atmosphere at 20 percent breathes in behind
it; a 3.5 percent dead-linear drift; fade over 18 frames.

The lockup's glyph extents are optically centred: the wordmark carries a right bearing, so
centre by glyphs, not by box.

The product film's own endcard is the one place a slogan appears beneath the lockup, at 34px
with the second sentence in `blue-light`. Everywhere else, the lockup alone.

Reduced motion shows the lockup complete.
