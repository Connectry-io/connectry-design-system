# Board

The board is the product's week view: what the brain found and did, on one sheet of glass, with
the brain's own message panel beside it. It is what opens when someone says "open my board", and
it docks beside the host's chat (see **HostChat**).

## Anatomy

- The app bar: the lockup at the left, the spaces as a rail at the right, the person's initial.
- The main sheet: the date and time as the eyebrow, the headline with one accent word in `blue`,
  **one** primary pill and one status chip ("Up since 05:10"), three numbers, then the rows.
- The message panel: a time as its label, one sentence in the brain's voice, a doc preview of what
  was drafted overnight, and the members row when the thing belongs to a circle.

## Numbers

Tabular, weight 500, with a muted line beneath that says what the number is about. Three at most.
They are the person's own numbers, never ours: renewals due, days until a trip, days left on a
document.

## Never

A second primary action, a chart, a settings link, a notification badge, a tile without a
status, empty glass.
