# SiteLasts

Chapter five: something that lasts. The one chapter with no glass; the picture carries it.

## Recipe

1. **Picture.** People passing something on (the Sunday call, grandparents, the cafe), full
   bleed, 720 high, the bottom scrim.
2. **Line.** "It passes on." 72px white, "on." in `blue-light`.
3. **Side.** "Yours, on your own keys. Delete a fact and it is gone. Take all of it with you, and
   name who it goes to. We never train on your data."
