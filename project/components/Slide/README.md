# Slide

16:9 at 1920 by 1080. Margin 86, lockup 36px top left, the section label in caps beneath it,
the page number bottom right, `ground` behind, glass only where a panel is needed.

**No bullet layouts.** One idea per slide, said in a headline and at most one paragraph.

## The eighteen layouts

Cover · agenda · divider · one idea · three points · proof · column chart · line chart · bar
chart before and after · part to whole · timeline · comparison · two column · quote · full
bleed with glass · full bleed with one line · team · closing.

The closing slide is the animated lockup, centred, no slogan. The cover carries the slogan
under the wordmark.

Charts are native and editable in the deck: accent series `chart-accent`, comparison
`chart-comparison`, further steps `chart-step-1` and `chart-step-2`, hairline gridlines only,
the value on the mark, a source line at the foot, one axis.

Type on a slide is the same scale as the web, one step up: display 120, headline 72, title 44,
body 28, small 22, label 20.
