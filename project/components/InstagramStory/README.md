# InstagramStory

Stories at 9:16, 1080 × 1920. The first card shows the two bands the app draws over a story.

- **Safe zones.** Nothing in the top 13 percent (the profile bar) or the bottom 17.7 percent (the
  reply bar). The footer, lockup with name and glass chip, lifts to 19 percent.
- **Line.** 11 percent of the width, in the lower middle or the middle; never over a face.
- **Panel.** One dark-plate panel at most, top left on the open third.
