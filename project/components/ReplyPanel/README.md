# ReplyPanel

The answer the brain gives beneath the ask bar: what was decided, why, and where it came from.

## Structure

- **Head.** The subject and the date it was decided as `label`; the source kind on the right
  (From the circle, From your mail).
- **Answer.** 17px `ink`, two sentences at most, specific: names, times, the choice made.
- **Why.** A well with the reason. This is the product's promise: it remembers why, not just what.
- **Sources.** Tags, one per source, each with its date.
- **Actions.** Up to two text links in the foot, never a button inside the panel.

Sits 14px above the **AskBar**, same width, same plate. Dark plate over dark or busy pictures.

## Never

An answer without its source, more than three tags, a confidence score, an assistant's name.
