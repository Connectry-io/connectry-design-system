# PhoneScreens

The phone variants of the glass components: the morning widget, the answer in the host, and
share to a circle as a bottom sheet.

## Rules at phone size

- Screen 390 by 844; panels sit 8 to 10px from the screen edge.
- Radius follows the device: 30 for a bottom sheet, 16 for a widget, 14 for cards in the host.
- Type steps down one size: titles 18, lines 13, labels 10 to 12 caps, never below 12px for text
  that must be read.
- Touch targets at least 44 by 44: the pill is full width in a sheet.
- Rows lose their state column when space runs out; the state moves into the line.

## Never

A phone frame with a named device, a status bar with a carrier's name, glass over the clock.
