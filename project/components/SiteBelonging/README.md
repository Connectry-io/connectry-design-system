# SiteBelonging

Chapter three: belonging. The circle, full bleed, speaking in its own panel.

## Recipe

1. **Picture.** The circle itself (five-a-side, flat share, the platform), full bleed, 720 high,
   the bottom scrim.
2. **Glass.** A **CircleCard** at 360px, top left on the open sky or wall, never over a face and
   never over the line: the line sits at the bottom, the panel at the top.
3. **Line.** "Some things are yours. Some things you share." 72px white, "share." in
   `blue-light`.
4. **Side.** "One memory for your people, a private layer for each of you. You share only what
   you choose."
