# AskHero

For film and campaign stills only; the site hero is **SiteHero**. The ask bar as the whole frame. A living film plate, the glass nav, one bar with
the person's own sentence, and one line back beneath it. Nothing else above the fold.

When the hero carries the ask, the headline rail stays empty: the two never share a frame.

## Build

The bar and the answer scale with the frame (`68.75cqw` wide, `7.5cqw` tall, the ask at
`2.5cqw`, the answer at `3.54cqw`); see **AskBar** for the full geometry and motion. The film
plate is one of the Film assets, looping only here because a site hero is not a film cut; it
keeps its poster frame under reduced motion.

## Pairs that work as a hero

- "Share the plan with the others" / "Shared. One copy each." over the flat share.
- "Is Dad's check-up still Thursday?" / "Thursday, 9:30. Leo's driving him." over the grandparents
  on a call.
- "What changed while I was asleep?" / "Three things. Already handled." over the dawn city.
- "Move the flight to Saturday" / "Checked. £38 more, same bags." over friends on the platform.

## Never

A second bar, a tagline where the answer goes, a boxed answer, a bar on a face, a CTA inside the
bar, a headline on top of the bar.
