# FilmLibrary

The product film, playable, with the seven frames that set its grammar. The clips it is cut
from are in **FilmClipsPeople**, **FilmClipsCircles** and **FilmClipsCity**; the files are in the
**Film** asset group.

- **The film.** 75 seconds, 16:9, sound on. One continuous music bed with no mutes or cuts: a
  soft dip into the halo at 0:59, a 3 dB swell onto the endcard from 1:10, then out with the
  picture. Speech rides over the bed; the endcard is the animated lockup alone.
- **Frames.** Overture in the kitchen, the board blooming open in the host, the phone host, the
  ask at the crossing and in the flat, the dashboard, the endcard.
- **Use.** On the site the film sits behind one play button, never autoplaying with sound. The
  hero uses the three-shot silent loop instead (see **SiteHero**).

Rules for the cut (hand-overs, defocus, the endcard, sound and loudness) are in the product film
section.
