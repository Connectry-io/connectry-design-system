# PhotoLine

The hero grammar: a photograph or a film frame, one line on the left rail, at most one glass
panel on the open third.

The line sits at the gutter, `5.6vw` from the bottom, at the film headline size with
`line-height` 1.04 and `-0.03em`. One accent word in `blue-light`. The second line is 0.42em,
weight 400, white at `opacity-second-line`.

Z order: photograph, `scrim`, glass, type. The scrim is 42 percent black rising from the
bottom over 55 percent of the height. Nothing sits above type.

## The interference rule

Type and glass never sit on a person's face. The line takes the open third; the panel takes
the open third beside the people. If a frame has no open third, choose another frame or
another crop. Shorten the copy before widening a panel.

The one exception is the AskBar, which may cross the body below the shoulders because it is
the person's own sentence.

## The photographs

Every scene is in the **Imagery** and **Circles** asset groups, each with an alt take. The
approved line-to-scene pairings are in the Imagery and film section; do not invent a new
pairing without checking it there.

## Accessibility

Film is `aria-hidden` with the line as real text beside it. A photograph carries a plain alt:
who, where, what they are doing. White on the 42 percent scrim clears 4.5:1 over every plate
in the set.

## Never

More than one line on a photograph. Centred type. Type on a face. A still where the site's
hero should be film.
