# WebSections

The glass moments of the website, in both editions, shown at rest. On the page each plays once when it scrolls into view.

- **Memory, an ask with a follow up.** A question types, the answer opens beneath it, the last word glows, then the follow up does the same. Width 440 at most, inside its film frame.
- **This morning.** Items arrive one by one; open items become Handled; one Needs you.
- **Circles.** Cards with photo, name, people count and one remembered line on desktop; the same as 56px rows on phones.
- **What each circle sees.** Toggles settle one by one; mail is always Only you, marked Never shared, with no toggle.
- **Where it lives.** Six sources by kind in one grid (three across, two on phones), a line drawn down, and "One memory, shared by the circle" spanning the grid. Sources are named by kind, never by product.
- **Yours.** Three promises: On your own keys, Take all of it with you, It passes on.
- **The sign-off.** "Remembers. Takes care. Passes on." as one line between two short hairlines.

Glass cards over film sit inside the frame and never hang over its edge.
