# TwoDoors

The two doors, described, never named as products. Two hairline columns with the rule in
`ink`, a `label` in caps, a `title` line beneath, and one short paragraph.

- **For you.** The individual, the whole of a life in scope, work included. "Your year,
  prepared."
- **For your people.** Circles. "Some things are yours. Some things you share."

Never "family" alone: a circle is a flat share, a five-a-side team, grandparents on a video
call, a parents' group, a couple, friends on a trip, the cafe, the freelancer, the small team.

There is no third door. No business door, no organisations, no pricing shape, no tiers.
