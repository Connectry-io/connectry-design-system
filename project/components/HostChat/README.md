# HostChat

Connectry inside the person's own assistant: the conversation stays in the host, the brain
answers through one connector and opens the board beside it.

## Structure

- **The host.** Replicated and toned: `film-host-page` behind, `film-host-paper` for cards and the
  composer, a 13 percent inset vignette. Never pure white, never a named assistant.
- **The reply.** A status line, then one line per connector with its verb in `blue` (read,
  moved, found, ready), then the summary with each subject in 600, then the board card (the
  mark on a `blue` tile, title, date).
- **The board.** Docked right on the person's picture: the headline panel with three numbers,
  the brain's message panel (dark plate), the week as rows (dark plate). Separate panels, never
  one inside another.

## Never

The host's brand, a vendor or assistant name, a chat of our own, white paper.
