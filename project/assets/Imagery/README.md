# Imagery

The individual: twenty scenes, each with an alternate take (`NN-scene.jpg` and
`NN-scene-alt.jpg`). One shoot, one grade: warm neutral, natural light, subtle grain, 35mm,
shallow depth, warm skin with a hint of cool blue in the shadows.

These carry the first door, *For you*. Each scene is approved for one line; the pairings are on
the **ImageryLibrary** page and in the Imagery and film section.

Compose with the subject in the right two thirds. The line takes the left rail; a glass panel
takes the open third beside the person, never their face.
