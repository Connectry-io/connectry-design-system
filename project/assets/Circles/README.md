# Circles

The second door, *For your people*: ten scenes of circles in all their forms. Grandparents on a
call, the flat share, five-a-side, the school gate, the Sunday sofa, the cafe opening, friends on
the platform, the toast, the freelancer, the small team.

Never a nuclear family alone as the picture of "your people". A circle panel on one of these
carries the members row and never a member's private memory.
