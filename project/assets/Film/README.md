# Film

**The product film.** `connectry-film-v15-720.mp4`, 75 seconds, 16:9, sound on: one continuous
music bed, the endcard lockup at the close. **FilmLibrary** plays it.

**The hero loop.** `connectry-hero-day-loop.mp4`, 8 seconds, silent, three shots (the kitchen,
the crossing, the toast) joined without a visible cut. The one loop in the system, behind the
site hero only (**SiteHero**); it keeps its first frame under reduced motion.

**Clips.** Five-second clips (`NN-scene.mp4`), 16:9, one slow camera move each and no cuts
inside a clip. The poster frame for each is the matching still in Imagery or Circles. They play
in **FilmClipsPeople**, **FilmClipsCircles** and **FilmClipsCity**.

`stills/` holds seven frames from the product film: the overture in the kitchen, the host chat
with the board blooming open, the endcard, the phone host, the ask at the crossing, the ask in
the flat, and the dashboard. They are the reference for the product components.

On film: hard cuts on the beat between clips, a rack-focus hand-over between two angles of one
panel, glass arriving after the first 0.4 seconds.
