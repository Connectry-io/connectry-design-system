# Connectry logo pack

Outlined masters from the Connectry design system v2.1. Transparent PNGs, vector SVGs.

| Folder | What | Use |
|---|---|---|
| lockup/ | the horizontal lockup (mark + wordmark) | ink on light grounds (default) · white on film, photographs and dark · blue only as a rare accent on white or #f5f5f7 |
| mark/ | the mark alone | favicon, app icon, avatars, notification sender tile |
| app-icon/ | the mark white on a rounded square | blue (default) or ink |
| avatar/ | the mark on a circle | blue, or ink on white |
| favicon/ | 64px SVG plus 16, 32, 48 PNG | browser tabs |

Colours: ink #1a1a1a · white #ffffff · blue #4a6fa5 · ground #f5f5f7.

Rules: clear space half the mark height on every side. Minimum size 96px or 24mm for the lockup, 16px or 4mm for the mark. Never stretch, rotate, outline, add a gradient or shadow, recolour beyond ink, white and blue, change the gap, animate the mark alone, or place the logo inside glass. There is no multi-colour version.
