# Connectry brand guide (paste into Moda: brand kit, Guides tab)

## Colour
- One accent: blue #4A6FA5. A primary action and one accent word, at most twice per surface.
- Everything else is the neutral ladder: ink #1A1A1A headlines, body #43444D running text, muted #5B5C66 captions, faint #80818D 12px caps labels only.
- Background is ground #F5F5F7; cards and inputs are surface #FFFFFF; rules are hairline #E4E4E8.
- Blue light #7FA3D6 only as the accent word on film or dark photography, never on white.
- Never: gradients as decoration, purple, navy fills, pure black backgrounds, glow, a second accent colour.

## Type
- Inter only. 400 for text, 500 for headlines, 600 for the wordmark only. Nothing else is bold.
- Headlines end with a full stop and carry one accent word at most, in blue.
- The only uppercase is the 12px label with +0.08em tracking.
- Body never below 17px on web, captions 13px, labels 12px.

## Layout
- Light first. One idea per surface. Generous space on a 4px scale.
- Radii: panels 16, cards 12, tiles 10. Buttons and fields are never pills on the website.
- Lists are hairline rows, not cards. Every grid row full.
- Slides: 1920x1080, margin 86, lockup 36px top left, label beneath, page number bottom right, no bullet layouts.

## Photography
- Real people in real moments, one person or one circle, natural light, warm neutral grade, subject in the right two thirds.
- Type and glass never sit on a face. The line goes on the open left third.
- Never: stock-looking AI imagery, illustrations, icon grids, posed smiles at camera, handshakes, a nuclear family alone as "your people".

## Glass
- Panels are light frosted glass (white 74 to 62 percent) over a photograph, with a 1px bright rim, never empty, never glass on glass, never the logo inside glass.

## Logo
- Default: the ink lockup on light grounds. White lockup on film, photography and dark. Blue lockup rarely, on white or ground only.
- Clear space half the mark height. Minimum 96px for the lockup, 16px for the mark.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, recolour, add effects, or place inside glass.

## Voice
- Sober, warm, specific. Short sentences. "It" more often than "we". Say what it did, not what it will do.
- Never: exclamation marks, em dashes, emoji, or the words delve, unlock, seamless, empower, journey, leverage, landscape, game-changing, supercharge, revolutionary, cutting edge, next generation, AI-powered, intelligent, smart.
- Never name an assistant or vendor: say "wherever you already work". Never "family" alone: the circle is always wider.
- Never on anything public: pricing, tiers, compliance claims, user counts, a specific industry.
- Lines that carry the voice: It takes care. · It remembers. · It holds your people. · Some things are yours. Some things you share. · Your year, prepared. · Passed down, not lost. · Wherever you already work.
